Category: Attitude control

Identifier Description
CTR-01 Hold the commanded attitude about the vertical axis to within ±1° for 60 s, and report the true drift rate over 10 min.
CTR-02 Rotate through a commanded 90° step, settling to within ±1° in under 5 s.
CTR-03 Overshoot on a 90° slew below 10 %.
CTR-04 Recover from a hand-imparted disturbance of 20–40 °/s (gyro-measured) to within ±1° in under 3 s.
CTR-05 Use a PID controller as the baseline controller.
CTR-06 Determine attitude from an onboard MEMS gyro only, with a full scale of at least ±250 °/s and bias calibrated at rest.
CTR-07 Complete at least 4 consecutive same-direction 90° slews, each meeting CTR-02.
CTR-08 At the wheel speed cap, limit wheel torque and flag saturation in telemetry, with no runaway.

Settling time is the time until the angle remains within ±1° of the target.

Category: Physical

Identifier Description
PHY-01 The rotating body fits a 1U CubeSat envelope (10 × 10 × 10 cm).
PHY-02 Rotating body mass ≤ 1.33 kg (CubeSat specification limit), allocated in a mass budget.
PHY-03 The body is supported on a single vertical axis: two bearings on a ground shaft, in an aluminium housing.
PHY-04 The overall centre of mass is within 2 mm of the rotation axis (CAD mass properties).
PHY-05 Bearing friction torque about the vertical axis ≤ 3 mN·m (spin-down test).
PHY-06 The rotation axis is vertical to within 0.1°.

Category: Power

Identifier Description
PWR-01 Average power ≤ 2 W while holding attitude with no disturbance (value confirmed after motor selection).
PWR-02 Runtime of at least 30 min on one charge.
PWR-03 Measure and report the energy used per 90° slew.
PWR-04 Onboard power from protected 18650 Li-ion batteries with a BMS.

Category: Thermal

Identifier Description
THM-01 Motor and driver IC temperature ≤ 60 °C after 10 min of repeated 90° slews (value confirmed after motor selection).
THM-02 Analyse the THM-01 temperatures with no convection (as in vacuum), including mitigations.

Category: Electronics and communications

Identifier Description
ELE-01 An ESP32-S3 module on a custom PCB as the microcontroller.
ELE-02 A BLDC motor driven by a driver on the PCB, with a magnetic encoder on the motor.
ELE-03 An all-in-one, 2-layer main PCB.
ELE-04 Wireless command and telemetry to a laptop over BLE, with telemetry of angle, rate, wheel speed, bus voltage and current at ≥ 50 Hz.
ELE-05 Battery bus voltage and current sensed on the PCB.

Category: Safety (from the risk assessment)

Identifier Description
SFT-01 A single key or button on the laptop sends a stop command that disables the motor.
SFT-02 The motor is disabled if the laptop heartbeat is lost for more than 0.5 s.
SFT-03 A latching power-isolation switch on the top face of the body isolates the battery from all loads.
SFT-04 A firmware hard limit on flywheel speed of ≤ 600 rpm.
SFT-05 The flywheel is enclosed in an aluminium enclosure (no acrylic or polycarbonate).
SFT-06 The flywheel is made of aluminium or brass.
SFT-07 A fuse at the battery pack terminals (rating set from measured peak current) and a firmware low-voltage cut-off.
SFT-08 The motor is enabled only after an arm command, with a firmware motor current limit.
SFT-09 Reverse-polarity protection, an input fuse and a regenerative over-voltage clamp on the PCB.

Category: Simulation

Identifier Description
SIM-01 The MATLAB model shows CTR-01 to CTR-04 are met before the hardware is built.

Category: Further objectives (stretch, not required for success)

Identifier Description
STR-01 An LQR controller compared against the PID controller.
STR-02 System identification of the rig, with a comparison of simulation against hardware.
STR-03 Magnetorquer momentum dumping modelled in MATLAB alongside the wheel.
STR-04 A PCB-trace magnetorquer prototype, with its dipole moment measured and fed back into the model.
STR-05 A multi-axis (2–3 wheel) extension, from the original brief (future work).
