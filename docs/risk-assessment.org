** Project description --- facts to write into ≥4 sentences (your words)
- Single-axis reaction wheel attitude-control demonstrator for a CubeSat-style satellite.
- A ~1U (10 cm cube, ≤1.33 kg) body rotates about a vertical axis on a low-friction bearing mounted
  on a fixed base clamped to the bench.
- A BLDC motor spins a metal flywheel (≤6000 rpm firmware limit) to rotate the body; controlled by
  an ESP32-S3 module on a custom PCB with a field-oriented-control motor driver and a MEMS gyro.
- Powered on the rig by an onboard protected 3S Li-ion pack (BMS + fuse), placed to balance the
  body. New boards are first brought up on a current-limited 12 V bench PSU.
- Commands and telemetry between the ESP32 and a laptop over its built-in wireless (Bluetooth LE or
  Wi-Fi); motor disabled automatically if the link drops. Physical power-isolation switch on the
  body.
- Work: PCB assembly (hand soldering, SMD pick-and-place after training), bench testing, operating
  the rig. PCB fabrication, laser cutting and 3D printing are done by technicians via fabrication
  requests.
