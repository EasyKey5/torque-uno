* What the motor has to do
| Input                    | Value                                                          | Source                                           |
|--------------------------+----------------------------------------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------|
| Minimum torque           | 8 mN·m                                                         | Earlier sim sizing; re-check after friction test |
| Speed limit              | ≤ 600 rpm                                                      | SFT-04                                           |
| Wheel                    | Brass Ø75 × 4.5 mm, 171 g, 1.2e-4 kg·m²                        | Earlier sim sizing                               |
| Wheel weight on motor    | 1.68 N                                                         | 0.171 kg × 9.81                                  |
| Friction to hold against | ≤ 3 mN·m                                                       | PHY-05                                           |
| Battery voltage          | 9.0–12.6 V (3S Li-ion)                                         | PWR-04; risk assessment                          |
| Pack fuse                | ≤ 5 A                                                          | SFT-07; risk assessment                          |
| Average holding power    | ≤ 2 W                                                          | PWR-01 (TBD)                                     |
| Motor temperature        | ≤ 60 °C after 10 min of 90° slews                              | THM-01 (TBD)                                     |
| Encoder                  | Magnetic, on the motor                                         | ELE-02                                           |
| Motor mass               | 80 g allocation                                                | Mass budget in requirements.org                  |
| Envelope                 | 100 × 100 mm footprint; 1U or 1.5U                             | PHY-01; decision due 9 Nov                       |
| Driver                   | DRV8316CRRGFR: 4.5–35 V, 8 A peak                              | TI datasheet SLVSH07                             |
| Control                  | Closed-loop voltage-mode FOC with encoder and wheel speed loop | Agreed working approach                          |

Where 8 mN·m comes from: body 2.0e-3 kg·m² × 1.2 rad/s² = 2.4 mN·m, plus 3 mN·m of friction, gives 5.4 mN·m. So 8 mN·m is about 1.5 times what a slew needs. At 8 mN·m the wheel reaches 600 rpm in 0.94 s. The body inertia and friction are still estimates.

* Datasheet checklist
Work down this list. The first group can rule a motor out quickly.

** Rule-out checks
| #  | Datasheet item               | Target                                                 | Why it matters                                                                      |
|----+------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1  | Datasheet and availability   | Full datasheet; in UK stock; active part               | Technicians won't order back-order items; eBay, Temu and AliExpress are not allowed |
| 2  | Speed constant (Kv or kn)    | Target 100–600 rpm/V                                   | Too low can't reach 600 rpm at 9 V; too high means more current for the same torque |
| 3  | Torque constant (Kt or kM)   | Target 16–95 mN·m/A                                    | Sets the current needed for torque; Kt = 9549 / Kv                                  |
| 4  | Rated (continuous) torque    | Target ≥ 8 mN·m; must be ≥ 3 mN·m                      | Must hold against friction without overheating and survive repeated slews           |
| 5  | Stall or peak torque         | ≥ 8 mN·m at 9 V and 600 rpm                            | Slews need this briefly; check it with the voltage calculation below                |
| 6  | Terminal resistance (R)      | Target ≥ 2.5 Ω                                         | Limits current in voltage mode: 12.6 V / 2.5 Ω = 5 A, the pack fuse rating          |
| 7  | Magnetic encoder or mounting | Built-in, or a rear or hollow shaft for a magnet       | ELE-02; Hall sensors alone don't meet it                                            |
| 8  | Height and diameter          | Target ≤ about 30 mm tall, including encoder and shaft | Decides 1U or 1.5U; confirm in CAD                                                  |
| 9  | Mass                         | Target ≤ 80 g net                                      | Mass budget; 210 g of margin exists, but the body limit is tight                    |
| 10 | Max axial bearing load       | Target ≥ 2 N                                           | The bearings carry the 1.68 N wheel on a vertical axis                              |

** Fine-tuning checks
| Datasheet item              | Target                                             | Why it matters                                                      |
|-----------------------------+----------------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Terminal inductance (L)     | Ripple below working current at your PWM frequency | Low L needs faster PWM, or ripple current wastes power              |
| Pole pairs (p)              | FOC update rate ≥ 20 × electrical frequency        | More pole pairs need a faster loop; rule of thumb                   |
| Rotor inertia               | Record it                                          | Adds to wheel inertia: the model uses J_flywheel + J_rotor          |
| Friction or no-load current | Target ≤ about 1 mN·m                              | Friction ≈ I0 × Kt; wastes torque and disturbs behaviour near 0 rpm |
| Cogging torque              | Smaller than friction; often unpublished           | Causes jerky torque when the wheel crosses 0 rpm                    |
| Thermal resistance          | Temperature rise keeps the case ≤ 60 °C            | THM-01                                                              |
| Back-EMF shape              | Sinusoidal preferred                               | FOC is smoothest with sinusoidal back-EMF                           |
| Bearing type                | Preloaded ball bearings                            | Low play and suits low-speed reversing                              |
| Shaft                       | Diameter, length, flat or hollow                   | Decides how the wheel attaches                                      |
| Connections                 | Plain wires or a usable connector                  | Some flat motors use flex cables that need an adapter board         |
| Rated voltage               | Not a limit by itself                              | A 24 V motor can work at 12 V if Kv still reaches 600 rpm at 9 V    |

* Calculations
Replace the values for each motor.
#+begin_example
Kt      [mN·m/A]  = 9549 / Kv                    if Kt isn't given
I_8     [A]       = 8 / Kt                       current at 8 mN·m
V_600   [V]       = 600 / Kv + I_8 × R           target ≤ 8 V (9 V pack, with margin)
P_8     [W]       = I_8² × R                     copper loss at 8 mN·m
P_3     [W]       = (3 / Kt)² × R                copper loss holding against friction
I_stall [A]       = 12.6 / R                     worst-case stalled current
Ripple  [A pk-pk] = 12.6 × 0.25 / (L × f_pwm)    simple worst-case estimate, L in H
f_e     [Hz]      = p × 600 / 60                 electrical frequency at the speed limit
Rise    [K]       = P × (Rth1 + Rth2)            add ambient; compare with 60 °C
#+end_example

Examples of motors these checks catch:
- *maxon EC 20 flat 351007:* 7.59 mN·m rated torque and 3.42 W at 8 mN·m.
- *DFRobot FIT1035:* passes, but needs 7.41 V at 600 rpm and 8 mN·m, so it has little headroom at 9 V.
- *maxon EC 45 flat 251601:* 1.03 Ω could draw 12 A at 12.6 V, and it weighs 110 g.

* Common traps
- *Torque labels:* "max output torque" usually isn't continuous torque. Find the rated value and check its conditions.
- *Torque units:* convert everything to mN·m.
  | Listed unit | In mN·m |
  |-------------+---------|
  | 1 g·cm      | 0.0981  |
  | 1 kg·cm     | 98.1    |
  | 1 N·cm      | 10      |
  | 1 oz·in     | 7.06    |
- *Resistance and inductance definitions:* values may be line-to-line, phase or winding. In delta, line-to-line R is 2/3 of winding R. The FIT1034 lists 5.1 Ω winding and 2.3 Ω phase, but 2/3 × 5.1 = 3.4 Ω, so measure it.
- *Kt conventions:* the same motor can give different answers. The FIT1034 gives 43 mN·m/A from Kv and 37 mN·m/A from flux, an 18% spread.
- *Rated speed vs no-load speed:* work out Kv from no-load speed. Rated speed is measured under load, so it understates Kv.
- *Gross mass:* "gross weight" usually includes packaging.
- *Distributor errors:* check listings against the manufacturer's datasheet. RS gives a 20 mm shaft diameter for a Ø32 mm motor (216-3787).
- *Rating conditions:* continuous ratings assume particular mounting and cooling, and a motor in an enclosure may run hotter. Faulhaber notes reduced thermal resistance for its rated values.
- *Bearing limits:* these are quoted at a stated speed and distance from the flange. For example, the Faulhaber 2214 allows 2 N axial at 3,000 rpm.
- *Very high Kt:* lower current is good for losses but makes it harder to measure. The DRV8316C has up to ±50 mA of offset before calibration, against 0.09 A at 8 mN·m on the FIT1035.

* Putting motor data into the Simulink model
Variables are in the model workspace of =MATLAB/my_bldc_controller.slx=. Conversions assume a star-equivalent model.

| Datasheet value  | Model variable | Conversion            | Note                                                        |
|------------------+----------------+-----------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------|
| Line-to-line R   | =Rs=           | R / 2                 | Measure the line-to-line value if the listing is unclear    |
| Line-to-line L   | =Ld=           | L / 2                 | Measure the line-to-line value if the listing is unclear    |
| Kt               | =psi_m=        | Kt / (1.5 × p)        | Approximate; Kt in N·m/A; confirm by measuring back-EMF     |
| Pole pairs       | Block setting  | p                     | Currently 1; recheck the sensor sector scaling if changed   |
| Rotor inertia    | =J_rotor=      | g·cm² × 1e-7 = kg·m²  | Adds to =J_flywheel= in Rotor Inertia                       |
| Dynamic friction | =B_rotor=      | To N·m·s/rad          | Convert from the datasheet's own units                      |
| Pack voltage     | =Vin=          | Test 9.0 V and 12.6 V | Currently fixed at 12 V                                     |
| PWM frequency    | =f_pwm=        | Target ≥ 20 kHz       | Currently 5 kHz, which gives too much ripple on real motors |

* Measure when the motor arrives
| Value            | How                                                  | Used for                     |
|------------------+------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------|
| R (line-to-line) | Low-resistance meter across each pair of wires       | =Rs=, losses, current limit  |
| L (line-to-line) | LCR meter across each pair of wires                  | =Ld=, PWM frequency          |
| Kt               | Spin it by hand or drill; measure back-EMF and speed | =psi_m=, torque              |
| Friction         | Spin-down test, with and without the wheel           | =B_rotor=, holding power     |
| Cogging          | Turn slowly by hand; log the encoder near 0 rpm      | Behaviour near 0 rpm         |
| Mass and size    | Scales and calipers                                  | Mass budget, CAD             |
| Bearing load     | Ask the supplier, or run a supervised spin test      | Whether it carries the wheel |
